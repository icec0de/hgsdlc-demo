# Web app - master spec

last increment: T-0000

## Requirements
### Requirement: Brand header
The page SHALL show the heading "Acme Coffee" and the tagline "Freshly roasted beans, delivered every Monday." centered in a white card on a warm beige background.
#### Scenario: page opens
- WHEN a visitor opens the page
- THEN the heading and the tagline are visible in the card

### Requirement: Order button
The page SHALL show an "Order now" button styled as a brown pill.
#### Scenario: order
- WHEN the visitor clicks "Order now"
- THEN the message "Thanks! Your order is on its way." appears below the buttons
